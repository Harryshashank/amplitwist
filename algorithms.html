Status badges are deliberate: deployed means it runs on real problems, research means it works in simulation, exploratory means we are still mapping it out.

Method

How we evaluate a candidate

A quantum approach earns a place here only after we have tried hard to beat it classically. The sequence is: formulate the problem, find the strongest classical baseline in the literature, implement the quantum mapping, simulate it noiselessly, then add a realistic noise model. Most candidates die at step four, and that result gets written up too.
